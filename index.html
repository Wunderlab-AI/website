A local-first AI workspace.

A curator plans the work, specialized agents carry it out, and everything runs on your own hardware, within limits you set.

Follow the project on GitHub Browse the organization

Specialists, not one giant model

Roles for conversation, research, coding, translation and more, coordinated by a curator and able to delegate to each other.

Yours to run and control

Runs on your own machine, with hard caps on how much of your CPU, memory and GPU it may use. Conversations stay stored locally.

Safe by design

Tools are sandboxed instead of agents, and changes to your files are reviewed and approved by you before they are applied.

Where things stand: Wunderlab is early. The design is described in the project README, and parts of it are not built yet. There is no release to download today; the repositories are the place to follow progress.
